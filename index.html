A legal associate that shows its reasoning and never invents the law.

The first of a suite of industry models built the Konomi way. A small model, trained on this laptop, reads a consumer's complaint. A verified library of real UK legislation works out the rights, the remedy and the deadlines. A gate lets nothing through that the library didn't give, and abstains when the model reports a critical fact missing. A qualified human reviews every word, and must: the small model's reading of the facts is the weak link, as the result below shows.

What this is

  
Simon's SLM Suite: one shared stack, and a model per industry where only the training data and the domain's rules change. Each finished one is both a showcase a prospect can test and the template for the next real client in that industry. Legal is first and built deep, because building it builds the shared stack.

  
This associate does the first screen on a consumer's complaint about goods, a service or an online purchase, under the law of England and Wales: which rights apply, which remedy, which deadlines matter, and an angle a busy reader might miss. It assists. A qualified human reviews it and owns any use of it.





  
Words on this page

  

    
SLMA small language model: here Qwen2.5 with half a billion parameters, small enough to train and run on a laptop.

    
LoRA, adapterA way to train a model by adding a small set of new weights (the adapter) instead of changing the whole model. The adapter is a single file of a few tens of megabytes.

    
Verified libraryThe associate's reasoning: rules written from the actual legislation, each checked against legislation.gov.uk, that compute the rights, the remedy and every deadline date.

    
The gateThe check between the model and the memo. Nothing reaches the memo unless the library gave it. If the model reports a critical fact missing, the associate abstains and says what it needs. If the model reads a fact that is not there, the gate cannot tell; the human review is what catches it.

    
AbstainDecline to answer, and say why. Better than a confident guess.

    
Held-outComplaints kept aside, written in different words from the training ones, and read once after everything was frozen.

    
Section 75Consumer Credit Act 1974, s. 75: when a single item costing over one hundred pounds and up to thirty thousand pounds is paid for by credit card, the card issuer can be liable too. A classic angle that gets missed.

    
Seal, mutation gateSeal: the claims, then the frozen model and code, committed to the public record before the held-out set is read. Mutation gate: a tool (witness) that breaks the code in small ways and checks the tests catch every break.

How it works

  

    
  

  
    
    
  1. Read. The trained model turns the complaint into facts: what was bought, how, the price, how it was paid, the dates, how many repairs failed, and whether the buyer simply changed their mind. It also writes a one-line summary.
    2. 
    
  2. Reason. The verified library takes those facts and works out the claim, the provisions that apply, the remedy and every deadline date, by fixed rules taken from the legislation.
    3. 
    
  3. Gate. If a fact the answer depends on is missing, it abstains and says which. A summary that mentions law or dates on its own is withheld. Every provision cited comes from the verified table.
    4. 
    
  4. Propose. Where an angle applies, such as section 75 against the card issuer, it is added, marked as a proposal.
    5. 
    
  5. Hand over. Every memo ends with the review label: a qualified human reviews it and owns any use of it.
    6. 
  





  
Blueprint

  

  
What is shared and what is legal: stack/ is the template every vertical reuses unchanged. verticals/legal/ is all that changes: the law table, the library's rules, the fact schema and the words.





  
What it will not do

  
    
    
  • It does not give legal advice, decide anything, file anything or send anything. A qualified human reviews it and owns any use of it.
    • 
    
  • It does not cite anything outside its verified table, and it does not compute a date any other way than the library's.
    • 
    
  • It does not guess a missing fact. It abstains and asks for it.
    • 
    
  • It covers one narrow area: consumer claims about goods, services and online purchases in England and Wales, under the provisions in its table. It is not a general legal tool.
    •

White paper: let the small model read and the law reason

  
The question. Can a model small enough to train on a laptop, wrapped in a verified library and a gate, triage real-shaped legal complaints better than a bigger model answering on its own, without ever inventing the law?

  
The method. Fourteen provisions read on legislation.gov.uk; a rule engine over them; synthetic complaints written from structured facts, 360 to train and 80 held out in phrasings never seen; a LoRA adapter on Qwen2.5-0.5B trained on the CPU; the claims and bars sealed first, the model and code frozen by hash, the held-out set read once.

    The limits, plainly:
    
  
      
    
    • Synthetic complaints, generated from templates. Real ones are messier.
      • 
    
    • One narrow area of consumer law, under fourteen provisions. No case law.
      • 
    
    • The bigger model stands in for a rented black box; it is a local qwen2.5:7b, not a commercial service.
      • 
    
    • A 0.5B model trained in a couple of hours on a laptop CPU; a GPU would allow a bigger one.
